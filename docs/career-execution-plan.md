### The Four Changes That Maximize Hiring Odds

1. **Pair applications with direct outreach (≥50% ratio).** Cold job-board forms yield 1–3% response. Pairing a tailored 1-page CV (`--focus fullstack|backend|frontend`) with a concise hiring-manager/founder/referral note linking `/work/rls-rehearsal` or `/work/docita` jumps response rates dramatically.
2. **Ship proof before sending more volume.** Publish `RLS Rehearsal`, `Retryglass`, and `Developer Pain Lab` on `buildora.work/work/[slug]` and replace aspirational Langfuse/Lightdash cards in `OpenSource.tsx` so every recruiter click lands on runnable senior proof.
3. **Run a structured 5-round interview rotation, not linear reading.** Spend 1 hour/weekday on the exact 5 Senior Full-Stack screens (JS/TS/React · Node/NestJS/Postgres/RLS · Timed DSA · System Design · Project Defense + STAR), linked directly to verified clean URLs on `study.buildora.work`.
4. **Enforce the 10-Application Diagnostic Gate.** If reply rate is under 10% across any 15-application cohort, pause and audit CV bullets + outreach messaging with a peer before sending the next batch.
