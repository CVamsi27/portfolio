# Daily workflow refinement

This refines the already approved daily-life design after feedback that the navbar reduction did not solve the overall UX. The chosen job remains running work, meals, exercise and reminders together.

The concrete problems are vertical stacking before useful actions, an empty task panel on a fresh day, and Health logging shortcuts separated from their corresponding records. Preserve all saved records, owner timetable restrictions, routine completion semantics and cloud planning gates.

Today keeps its next-action recommendation, then exposes dated recording controls before the agenda. The agenda and pending task queue share a desktop row; mobile remains one readable column. Omit the task queue when empty on Today, retaining task creation in Plan and Add. Time zones and reminder administration use an explicit disclosure after the records. Smaller shared chapter headers reduce repeated introductory space without removing page headings or contextual back links.

Health pairs status with actions on each record row. Food has Add food and its diary; Exercise has recording and its session; Body has weigh-in and history; Sleep has one recovery action; Water retains one-click increment and Undo. Remove the separate duplicate shortcut row. All capture links carry the displayed day and return to Health.

Use the existing colors and type system. Treat this as a workflow correction, not proof that every remaining page is ideal. Verify populated and empty states, narrow screens, historical dates, task creation, water Undo and clock behavior. Keep the public portfolio unchanged.
